India Document Hierarchy & Workflow

Root: India 🇮🇳
Main Section: Dose Information / Document Information (All Ranks Supported)
1. Travel Docs
  • Passport: (Mandatory) Primary national identity & travel document.
  • CDC: (Mandatory) Continuous Discharge Certificate seafarer service book.
  • SID: (Mandatory) Seafarers' Identity Document (biometric maritime identity card).
  • US Visa: (Optional) US C1/D transit/crew visa or B1/B2 visa.
  • Resume / CV: (Optional) Seafarer curriculum vitae and sea-service experience profile.
2. Medical Docs
  • ILO Medical: (Mandatory) International Labour Organization seafarer fitness certificate.
  • COVID Vaccine: Double-dose / booster vaccination record.
  • Yellow Fever: International vaccination certificate for maritime zones.
3. Flag State docs (Rankings for all states & ranks)
  • State Rankings: Panama (#1), Liberia (#2), Marshall Islands (#3), Bahamas (#4), Malta (#5), Cyprus (#6), Singapore (#7), India MMD (#8), Isle of Man (#9), Hong Kong (#10).
  • Supported Ranks: Master, Chief Officer, 2nd Officer, 3rd Officer, Chief Engineer, 2E, 3E, 4E, ETO, Bosun, AB, OS, Cook, Messman, etc.
